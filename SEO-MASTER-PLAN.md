# BANGLA LED — SEO, CRO & Brand Master Plan

The grand plan for dominating the digital billboard / LED billboard / billboard advertising search space in Bangladesh — and converting that visibility into booked placements.

---

## 1. Competitive Landscape (How the Sector Searches Today)

The Bangladesh OOH search market splits into three tiers, and the gap between them is the opportunity:

| Tier | Who | How they show up | Weakness to exploit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Legacy OOH agencies** | Traditional billboard agencies and ad firms | Thin brochure sites, no location-level pages, stock photography, contact-form-only | Zero programmatic depth — they rank (weakly) on 2–3 head terms and nothing else |
| **Directory / marketplace listings** | Business directories, B2B marketplaces, Facebook pages | Aggregated listings with no data, no pricing logic, no audience intelligence | They win long-tail queries by accident, not design — easily outranked by genuinely helpful location pages |
| **DOOH operators** | A small number of modern LED screen operators | Better visuals, but still site-wide generic copy; one "Our Screens" page for the entire network | No URL per placement → no entity for Google to rank for "billboard at {landmark}" queries |

**The core insight:** nobody in the market owns the *location-level* query space — "billboard at Police Plaza", "LED screen Gulshan", "digital billboard near Dhaka airport". These queries have extreme commercial intent (the searcher already knows where they want to be seen) and effectively zero competition. The theme is engineered to own them.

## 3. Keyword & Silo Architecture (Zero Cannibalization)

One page per intent. No two pages chase the same query.

```
HOME  →  brand + category head terms
│        "digital billboard advertising Bangladesh", "DOOH network Bangladesh"
│
├── /locations/  (CPT archive) → inventory terms
│        "LED billboard locations Dhaka", "digital billboard rental Dhaka"
│
├── /billboards/{city}/  (City taxonomy) → city silo hubs
│        "billboard advertising in Dhaka", "digital screens Chattogram"
│
├── /area/{neighborhood}/  (Neighborhood taxonomy) → district pages
│        "billboard Gulshan", "LED screen advertising Banani"
│
├── /locations/{placement}/  (single-location) → landmark long-tail ★ the moat
│        "billboard at Police Plaza", "digital screen Kamal Ataturk Avenue"
│
└── /campaigns/  → proof + brand-comparison queries
         "OOH campaign case study Bangladesh", "billboard campaign examples Dhaka"
```

**LSI vocabulary discipline** (already baked into the templates): rotate *DOOH network, digital airspace, premium media placement, share of voice, cinema-grade fidelity, placement, screen, corridor* — never repeat the head term per page beyond H1 + one body mention. This satisfies helpful-content signals and prevents internal competition.

**Structured data** (shipped in the theme): `Organization` sitewide, `Place` + `GeoCoordinates` on each location, `BreadcrumbList` reinforcing the silo. This is what gets location pages into map-adjacent and "near me" results.

### Programmatic SEO playbook
Every new screen = one new Location post (5 minutes of admin work) = one new indexable, conversion-ready landing page with unique data points. Scale rule: **every placement gets 150+ words of unique editor copy** (the corridor, the audience, the moment) — data fields alone are not enough for helpful-content compliance.

Content calendar to feed the silo (2 posts/month):
- "The complete guide to billboard advertising rates in Dhaka" (rates queries = highest intent)
- "DOOH vs static billboards in Bangladesh: the data"
- "How dwell time works: why signalised intersections outperform highways"
- Per-city guides as the network expands ("Billboard advertising in Chattogram: corridors that matter")

## 4. Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

The funnel is built into the theme; this is the operating doctrine:

1. **Above-the-fold CTA everywhere** — every page type ends in (and the nav always offers) a route to `#booking`. The glass "BOOK NOW" button never leaves the viewport.
2. **Scarcity framing, honestly used** — "Availability is strictly limited / placements sell by the quarter" is true for premium OOH; say it on every form.
3. **The media kit is the lead magnet** — never ask for a "contact"; offer an asset (media kit, availability calendar, rate structure). The ask is reframed as access, matching the exclusive brand voice ("Join the Exclusive List").
4. **Per-location forms** — the single-location form auto-attaches the placement name to the lead, so sales replies with a specific availability answer, not a generic brochure. Specific replies close.
5. **Low-friction fields** — Name, Brand, Email, Dates. No phone requirement, no dropdowns. Every removed field measurably lifts B2B form completion.
6. **Leads are never lost** — every submission is stored as a private Lead post *and* emailed. Follow-up SLA: one business day (the form promises it; honor it).
7. **Measure**: GA4 events on form submit (`lead=success` URL is the trigger), scroll depth on location pages, and CTA click-through from hero vs. footer. Iterate creative on the hero CTA copy quarterly.

- **Citations** — consistent NAP across Bangladesh business directories, ad-industry listings, and chamber-of-commerce entries.
- **Digital PR** — every major campaign launch is a press moment ("X brand takes over Gulshan's digital airspace"). Trade press + business dailies link back to the campaign case page.
- **Client logos & case studies** — each case study page is a link-earning asset; clients link to their own campaign write-ups.

## 6. KPIs & 90-Day Roadmap

**KPIs:** indexed location pages, non-brand organic clicks to /locations/*, media-kit requests/month, lead→proposal rate, share of voice on the 10 head terms (tracked via rank monitoring).

- **Days 1–14** — Launch theme, submit XML sitemap, create GBP, publish all current placements as Location posts with unique copy and real photography.
- **Days 15–45** — Publish the rates guide + DOOH-vs-static post; build citations; wire GA4 + Search Console; first PR push.
- **Days 46–90** — Expand neighborhood coverage (every placement tagged and every term page given intro copy); publish 2 campaign case studies; begin quarterly CRO review of hero CTA and form conversion.

The structural advantage compounds: every screen added to the network automatically widens the search moat. Competitors would need to rebuild their entire web presence to follow.
